# Analyze & Optimize

Run **Token Optimizer: Analyze & Optimize Repo** to:

1. **Analyze** your repo (structure, stack, noise, semantic-index source) — deterministically.
2. **Recommend** prioritized token-efficiency levers with a 0–100 score.
3. *(Optional)* **Tailor** the instructions with Copilot — consent-gated, uses your Copilot quota.
4. **Preview** every change as a diff.
5. **Apply** on approval (optionally to a new branch). Re-runs are idempotent.

Nothing is written without your explicit approval.
